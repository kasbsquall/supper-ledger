# Supper Ledger

## Purpose
The night-of ledger for a benefit dinner. The organizer pastes the family group chat, and the page shows money collected against the goal, plates to cook per pickup slot, and who still owes. Source: `devpost/scope.md`, `devpost/prd.md`.

## Audience
The relative who ends up running a benefit dinner (a spaghetti dinner for a hospital bill, the North American cousin of the Peruvian pollada pro-fondos). Often not technical. Uses it on a phone, standing at a folding pickup table in a church hall, school cafeteria, or driveway, with a line of people waiting. Secondary audience: hackathon judges at Devpost (North America) opening the live link on a laptop.

## Voice
Warm, plain, and brief. Talks like a helpful cousin with a clipboard: "Still owes $45", "6:00 pm: 18 plates". Never fintech or corporate. No exclamation marks.

## Constraints
- Phone first, one hand, readable at arm's length; also good on a laptop for the demo.
- English by default, Spanish toggle (USD and Venmo, or soles and Yape).
- No account, no server; data stays on the device.
- Public repo: OFL fonts only. Icons: Phosphor Light. No emoji. No Inter, Roboto, system fonts.
- Tabular figures everywhere.

## Real proof
Sample dinner (clearly labeled): "Rivera Family Spaghetti Dinner", $15 a plate, goal $3,000, pickup 5:00, 6:00, 7:00 pm. Chat lines like "Aunt Linda 5 tickets, sent 2 on Venmo" and "Marcus 3 plates, pickup 6pm, will cash at the door".

## Key screen
The board with the sample dinner loaded: goal figure, plates per pickup slot, paste box with the last paste result, and the ledger rows (owes / paid / picked up).
