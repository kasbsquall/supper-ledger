Supper Ledger

One paste, and the night adds up.

When a family sells plates to cover a hospital bill, the orders arrive as group-chat messages, and on the night one relative scrolls back and adds it all up by hand. Supper Ledger reads the pasted chat and shows money collected against the goal, plates to cook for each pickup time, and who still owes.

Try it in a minute: press Try the sample dinner, then Add to ledger, and watch the thermometer climb from $1,650 to $1,995.

It reads lines like "Aunt Linda 5 tickets, sent 2 on Venmo" or "Tía Rosa 4 platos, yapeó 2", shows back any line it cannot read, and keeps everything in the browser on the organizer's phone. For those lines the organizer can ask AI with their own key, and confirms each suggestion before it counts. No sign-up, no server. English and Spanish.
